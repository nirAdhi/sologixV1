PM Surya Ghar: Mufti Bijli Yojana

The PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana is India's flagship rooftop solar scheme launched to make solar energy affordable and accessible for residential households. Under this initiative, eligible households can receive benefits of up to 300 units of free electricity every month along with direct government subsidy support.
